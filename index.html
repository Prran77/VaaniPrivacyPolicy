1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, track, or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts). All application data and user preferences are stored strictly locally on your own mobile device.

2. Device Permissions & Microphone Access

To provide its core functionality, the Application requires access to your device's Microphone. This permission is used solely to capture your voice input and transform it into text.

  • Local Processing: All audio processing and speech-to-text conversion are executed locally on your device.
  • No Audio Retention: Your voice recordings are never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any external server or third party.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the Application does not collect or transmit any user data to external servers, the Service Provider does not host, store, or maintain any of your data. You have complete control over your local data, which can be entirely cleared at any time by uninstalling the Application or clearing the app cache via your Android system settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided personal information to a third-party service used within this app, please consult the respective third-party policies or contact us.
